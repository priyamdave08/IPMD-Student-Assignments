Made of sound.

Recorded tracks come from the music library: Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Each result shows its track\u2019s credit and the full list is in music-library/CREDITS.md. Tracks are shortened and faded to fit the video.

Visual analysis uses only your selected sphere interior. The source video’s audio is excluded. Generated music is processed by the selected local engine.

Instrument recordings

tonejs-instruments — Nathaniel Brosowsky, CC BY 3.0. Piano and violin: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios. Cello: flcellogrl, Freesound. Electric guitar: Karoryfer. Recordings are sequenced, pitch shifted, enveloped and mixed.
