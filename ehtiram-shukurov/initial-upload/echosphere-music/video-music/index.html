EchoSphere · runs in your browserMake music from a feeling instead

Made of light and sound.

Your video is opened and read inside this page. It is never uploaded. The sphere is found from its rim, its lit and coloured interior and its movement, then its colours give the feeling: gold is Warm, violet is Calm, blue is Sad, red or orange is Dynamic. That is a colour rule, not a trained model, so it can be wrong, and you can always choose the feeling yourself.
